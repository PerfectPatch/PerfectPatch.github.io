date: 2026-10-02

I've been thinking about building my own web app for a long time, but the project itself is serious work that's going to take real effort. I don't really know backend at all, and I want to learn it properly, maybe even to a professional level.

I do use AI tools for development, of course, but I'm stepping away from vibe coding. It's not even about the risk of not understanding my own project. Anyone willing to put in the time can stitch something together blindly. It's a different thing when you actually know the subject and understand how everything works. I've got a ton of work ahead of me.

Until now, I took a pretty shallow view of web development and thought it ended at HTML and CSS. I was very wrong, and now I see how huge it really is. I'm not sure how I'll keep up with it all: on top of university, I have to learn a whole industry, where people talk at a professional level, not at the level of HTML and CSS questions.

I don't know yet where I'll be looking for information. Honestly, I think the best approach is the university one. University doesn't hold your hand. It just shows you what you need to master right now, and how you get there is up to you. Your only job is to actually learn it. I'll probably also look on Reddit and forums. Sure, there are typical online courses on platforms like Udemy, but that's exactly where they hold your hand. That's great for complete beginners, but if you boil those courses down, they mostly cover the very beginning and don't go deep into how things work professionally.
