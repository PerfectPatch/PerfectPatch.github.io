## Background

I kept learning the software. I made a rough model of the cardioid microphone capsule I'm waiting for from eBay, for my project that hasn't started yet.

## Problems

FreeCAD is a temperamental program. I don't know about the others. Every time I undo the previous operation, the program freezes, so I constantly have to wait for it to come back to life. I won't jump to conclusions yet, since I think I'm just making beginner mistakes.

## Results

Here you can see an approximate model of the 20 mm capsule. I didn't know how to make the mesh grille, and I figured it would be unnecessary anyway, since I wasn't really modeling the microphone itself, just a placeholder so I can design the body around it later.
